<p>Have your child / you been inspired by a teacher's care or a staff member's
creativity?</p>
<p>The Caring Teacher and Innovative Staff Awards are now open for nominations!</p>
<p>To nominate, simply scan the QR code or click on the link—it'll take you
straight to our Form.sg nomination form. Super quick and easy!</p>
<p>Don't hold back your appreciation—let us know who makes your/child's day
brighter! Just remember, nominations close on the 23rd of August.</p>
<p>Get your entries in now, and let's celebrate our EVG heroes together!
We are so excited to see who you nominate!</p>
